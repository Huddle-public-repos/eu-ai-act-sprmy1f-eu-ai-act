# EU AI Act requirements for meeting-notes startup · research

**TL;DR:** The EU AI Act imposes transparency obligations on meeting-notes startups that use AI to generate content or interact with users, effective August 2, 2026, including clear disclosure that users are interacting with AI and machine-readable marking of AI-generated content.

## Answer

The EU AI Act, effective from August 2, 2026, imposes specific transparency obligations on providers and deployers of AI systems, including those used in meeting-notes startups. Key requirements relevant to meeting-notes tools include:

1. **Disclosure of AI Interaction**: Providers must design AI systems that interact directly with users (e.g., chatbots, virtual assistants) to clearly inform users they are engaging with AI, unless this is obvious from context.
2. **Machine-Readable Marking of AI-Generated Content**: Providers of AI systems that generate synthetic content (text, audio, video) must mark outputs in a machine-readable format to indicate they are AI-generated or manipulated. This applies to meeting notes generated by AI.
3. **Deployer Obligations**: If a deployer uses AI for emotion recognition, biometric categorization, or creates deepfakes, they must inform affected individuals. While less directly applicable to standard meeting-notes, any such features would trigger these rules.

## Key findings

- **Transparency Deadline**: Most transparency obligations under Article 50 of the AI Act became enforceable on August 2, 2026.
- **Scope**: Applies to any AI system placed on the EU market or whose outputs are used in the EU, regardless of the provider's location.
- **Interactive Systems**: Chatbots or AI agents used in meeting-notes must disclose their nature to users.
- **Content Marking**: AI-generated meeting summaries, transcripts, or other content must include machine-readable markers.
- **Exemptions**: Certain uses, such as research & development or personal non-professional activity, may be exempt.

## Implications for the team

- **Product Design**: Ensure any user-facing AI components (e.g., chat assistants) clearly indicate they are AI.
- **Content Generation**: Implement machine-readable markers (e.g., metadata, watermarks) for AI-generated meeting notes or summaries.
- **Compliance Monitoring**: Track any new features that fall under emotion recognition, biometric categorization, or deepfake creation, as these have additional disclosure requirements.
- **Documentation**: Maintain records of compliance measures, especially around content marking and user disclosures, to prepare for potential audits or procurement questionnaires.
